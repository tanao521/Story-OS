# Simulator visual direction

## Chosen direction — Night editorial desk / evidence rail

**Design proposition:** a chapter review should feel like an editor's marked-up night desk: the authoritative read is the paper under the lamp, model interpretation is a separate annotation margin, and the audit rail is the ledger that makes provenance inspectable.

Keywords: restrained, nocturnal, editorial, ruled, evidentiary, high-density.

Palette inherits Story OS dark layers, gold `#bca374` for authority, violet `#7c6cf2` for model supplement/interaction, green/warn/red/info status tokens, and hairline borders. Typography uses the existing editorial serif for chapter/persona headings, body sans for controls, and mono for ids/counters. The signature is a narrow gold rule and `◆ DETERMINISTIC` stamp on the authority surface, echoed by the right-hand selection/evidence ledger.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Broadsheet audit:** strong rules and multi-column density; rejected because it would overfit conflict/evidence and reduce mobile quality.
2. **Quiet manuscript:** warm paper surfaces and serif-first hierarchy; rejected because it would sever the dark Story OS shell and make status semantics less immediate.

The selected direction is the only one implemented in the prototype. It avoids cyberpunk neon, glass, gradients-as-content, marketing hero blocks, and generic dashboard mosaics. Motion is limited to existing transitions; reduced motion disables it.
